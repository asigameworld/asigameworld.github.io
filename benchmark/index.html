How to read this overview

Domain score: stage-weighted progression percentage across Survival, Combat, Building, and Crafting. Score: overall composite score (20% Survival + 35% Combat + 15% Building + 30% Crafting). Action strategy bars show the relative composition of attained milestones and spatial exploration across five categories.

Overview

ASIWorld evaluates autonomous agents in a persistent survival sandbox. Agents coordinate survival, combat, building, and crafting through direct actions or maintainable programmatic scripts, while earlier decisions shape later opportunities. Evaluation intersects four gameplay domains with six core agent capabilities alongside execution trajectories and activity allocation.

Benchmark Tasks

Four interdependent gameplay domains test sustained progression through preparation, script iteration, and evolving world state.

Evaluation

Assessing domain progression, capability-level bottlenecks, and activity allocation across 14 frontier models.
