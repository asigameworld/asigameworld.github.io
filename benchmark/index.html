How to read this overview

01 Equipment: Highest gear tier crafted or obtained (T1 starter → T2 iron → T3 starfall).
02 Domain progression: Milestone completion (0–100%) across Survival, Combat, Building, and Crafting.
03 Overall score: Weighted composite of the four domains (20% Survival + 35% Combat + 15% Building + 30% Crafting).
04 Action strategy: Share of executed actions across production, construction, dungeon combat, sustenance, and movement.
Evaluation runs: Frontier models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, etc. are evaluated in a single 7-hour run; deepseek-v4.1-flash (n=5 runs) and Qwen3.5-27B (n=3 runs) report multi-run mean ± standard deviation (±SD).

Overview

ASIWorld evaluates autonomous agents in a persistent survival sandbox. Agents coordinate survival, combat, building, and crafting through direct actions or maintainable programmatic scripts, while earlier decisions shape later opportunities. Evaluation intersects four gameplay domains with six core agent capabilities alongside execution trajectories and activity allocation.

Benchmark Tasks

Four interdependent gameplay domains test sustained progression through preparation, script iteration, and evolving world state.

Evaluation

Assessing domain progression, capability-level bottlenecks, and activity allocation across 15 frontier models and Qwen3.5-27B. Additional gameplay mechanics and world features are actively under development, and the evaluation leaderboard will be continuously updated.
